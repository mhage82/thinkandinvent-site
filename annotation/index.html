Labeling guide & examples

Identify visible image defects and separately rate scene usability. Apply multiple labels when multiple artifacts are visible. Do not discuss answers with other annotators before returning your work.

  1. Enter the annotator code and group assigned by the organizer.
  2. Inspect the image; use Enlarge image when needed. Select all visible artifacts, or explicitly choose no listed artifact / unsure.
  3. Rate usability. “Usable” means you can recognize and describe the main scene and important objects; it does not mean flawless.
  4. Your selections save automatically. Click Next to continue. Previous and the image selector let you review answers. Resume using the same browser, code, and group.
  5. Click Save results at any time to download your selections and send it using the instructions below. CSV is an optional readable copy.

Borderline cases

A few lens droplets on an otherwise sharp scene: Lens contamination and Usable. A finger at the edge: Partial obstruction and Usable if important content remains visible. Light fog can be Usable; heavy fog may be Partly usable or Unusable. Add Fog and Lens contamination together only when both a veil and distinct spots / smears are visible.

“No listed artifact visible” and “Unsure” are exclusive choices. If you confidently see one artifact but are unsure about another, select the labels you are confident about.
